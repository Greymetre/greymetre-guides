Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your details. They are processed by the service providers we use to send email and host this site, only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we are in touch about our services, and delete them when you ask us to or when they are no longer needed.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent, ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it at any time. Write to anadi@greymetre.com and we will act on it within 30 days. Every email we send also lets you reply to stop further contact.
